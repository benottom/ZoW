Mid-mission, the drone's reported position stops matching its expected flight path. Is this sensor drift, an environmental effect, or something actively interfering with navigation?

You work through the available telemetry and sensor cross-checks to characterize the anomaly, then decide when to trust onboard fallback behavior versus intervening manually. The mission rewards a documented, defensible decision over a fast guess.

Contested Navigation is a foundational scenario for building the pattern-recognition skills used across ZoneOfWar's more advanced navigation-integrity missions.
