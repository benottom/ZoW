Two teams, one mission, live consequences. Contested Airspace Exercise puts a Red Team and a Blue Team into the same simulated UAS operation at the same time. Every Red Team action changes the state of the mission, and every Blue Team response is scored against it.

The exercise runs through ZoneOfWar's standard Red vs Blue flow: briefing, preparation, live exercise with instructor-controlled injects, and a shared debrief covering the full timeline, score breakdown and root causes.

This mission is instructor-led and best suited to cybersecurity teams, university competitions and structured pilot programs.
