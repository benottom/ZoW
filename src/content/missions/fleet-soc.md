You're staffing a simulated fleet security-operations desk covering multiple UAS assets across concurrent missions. Alerts are arriving faster than any one person can chase — the job is to prioritize by mission impact, not alert volume.

Fleet SOC is designed as a capstone-style team mission that pulls together telemetry monitoring, anomaly detection, incident triage and mission-impact reasoning from earlier missions in the Blue Team path.

Recommended after: Navigation Integrity, Fleet Intrusion.
