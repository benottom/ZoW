One drone in a small fleet starts behaving inconsistently with its assigned mission profile. The rest of the fleet appears normal — for now.

Working as a team, you correlate telemetry, fleet-management logs and network activity to scope the incident, decide which asset needs to be isolated, and contain the problem without grounding missions that do not need to stop. The scenario rewards precise scoping: over-containment costs mission availability, under-containment risks the fleet.

Fleet Intrusion is one of ZoneOfWar's team-oriented missions, designed to be run with shared visibility and a debrief at the end.
