Telemetry shows inconsistent position behavior during a critical autonomous mission. Investigate the evidence, preserve safe operation and document the root cause.

This mission builds directly on the pattern-recognition skills introduced in Contested Navigation, adding a full evidence trail and a formal after-action write-up. You'll work through positioning telemetry, sensor cross-checks and mission logs to isolate the root cause, apply the right degraded-mode fallback, and produce documentation a team lead could act on.

Recommended next mission after completion: Fleet SOC.
