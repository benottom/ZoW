As mission telemetry becomes intermittent, you have to decide what to trust, what to verify, and what to act on immediately. This mission is built around a realistic problem defenders face constantly: signal loss is common, but it is also the first symptom of several serious problems.

You'll work through a stream of partial, sometimes contradictory telemetry and practice separating routine degradation from evidence that deserves escalation, all while the mission clock keeps running.

Telemetry Under Pressure pairs well with Navigation Integrity and Contested Navigation as part of a detection-focused mission set.
