A simulated UAS starts behaving in a way that doesn't match its documented configuration. Nothing was reported as changed — but something clearly is different.

You investigate firmware, configuration and third-party component history inside the sandbox to trace the discrepancy back to its source, then frame the finding the way a product-security engineer would communicate it to an engineering team: what changed, why it matters, and how to prevent recurrence.

Supply Chain Surprise is an advanced mission in the UAS Red Team Practitioner and UAS Product Security Engineer learning paths.
