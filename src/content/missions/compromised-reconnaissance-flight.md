A reconnaissance drone is completing a routine survey when ground-control logs show access patterns that do not match the assigned operator. You need to work out fast whether this is a benign anomaly, a data-confidentiality problem, or the early stage of a command-integrity compromise.

The mission workspace gives you telemetry, ground-control application logs and cloud access records. Your job is to correlate them, decide what evidence to preserve, and choose the response that protects the mission without triggering an unnecessary abort.

Scoring rewards accurate detection and clean evidence handling over speed alone — a wrong containment call costs more than a few extra seconds of investigation.
